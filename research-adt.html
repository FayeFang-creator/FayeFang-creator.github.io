BMW Assisted Driving Simulator

To enhance trust and safety in BMW assisted-driving scenarios, we as BMW Innovation Team developed an in-vehicle gamified tutorial using Unreal Engine 5. Prototyped within an actual car environment based on the BMW iDrive8 system, this gamified tutorial integrates multimodal interactions—including touchscreen controls, acoustic feedback, steering, braking, and acceleration—to familiarize users with BMW Assisted Driving functionalities in an engaging and immersive manner.

touchpoints

Entire Car As "Game Room"

The gamified tutorial was developed as in-car game, designed and prototyped on four key points: development entirely with Unreal5 for seamless hardware and software integration; a real car demo for tangible interaction and user test; an ADAS tutorial to build user knowledge and confidence; and a 3D gamified demo that enhances learning with an immersive, enjoyable simulation.

product roadmap

Start From Beginner Level

Adopting a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) approach, we initiated our validation by assessing technical and design feasibility. The current scope focuses on ADM(Assisted Driving Mode)—specifically ACC(Adaptive Cruise Control) and  LKA(Lane-Keeping Assistance)—as fundamental functionalities of Level 2 ADAS and primary points of user interaction upon system activation. Additional essential modules, such as Lane-Change Assistance, Automated Lane Change, Parking Assistance, and Automated Valet Parking, etc, will be explored in subsequent phases contingent upon confirmed feasibility and demonstrated user value.

USER RESEARCH

"Why You Never Used BMW ADAS Functions?"

"In Which Case Would You Like To Use ADAS?"

We identified pain points in ADAS learning through a lab-based user study (N = 15) with experienced drivers hesitant to use ADAS. Participants cited a lack of confidence and safety concerns, especially at high speeds, and were overwhelmed by the complexity of ADAS features. These insights shaped our strategy to develop a gamified tutorial that builds trust and eases anxiety pre-learning. By offering an engaging, interactive practice rather than traditional slides, users learn ADAS basics through fun, level-by-level challenges, gradually gaining confidence.

Game Design

In-car Tutorial Game Design

As lead UX designer and game designer, I created the mechanics and level design, complete with storyboard and a user flow that guides players through learning BMW's assisted driving features. Players will navigate challenges step-by-step in a car through four immersive missions, starting with manual driving and advancing to activating assisted driving, speed control, and managing the system's activation and deactivation.

USER JOURNEY

"Learn Through Practice"

We conducted an in-depth analysis of numerous Chinese and global vehicles, Our design strategy centers on learning through practice. We've structured the user journey by dividing Assisted Driving Mode (ADM) tasks into levels. Users start with a foundational ADM overview and then advance through essential BMW training modules, completing successive missions.

INFO ARCHITECTURE

The product's main interface is a 3D game view in Unreal Engine, featuring a seamless driving experience with a simulated environment, key traffic objects, and the ego car. It includes a combined speedometer and ADAS icon, along with an ADAS status indicator. A tutorial video guides user actions, and voice guidance offers explanations and support throughout the experience.

MOCK-UP

Mission 0 : Starter Plaza

In the first training level, users learn to activate Assisted Driving Mode (ADM) from manual driving. Upon hands-on detection, a countdown prompts pedal use. When reaching higher speeds, the level triggers, followed by a celebratory notification and smooth transition.

Mission 1: Activate Assisted Driving Mode

Users can effortlessly transition to the next level by clicking a button, where they will learn to activate Assisted Driving Mode (ADM) while driving at high speeds. Guided by voice instructions and a tutorial video, users will encounter animations detailing ADM operations, including Adaptive Cruise Control and Lane Keeping Assistance.

Mission 2: Speed Control

Subsequent levels follow a similar structure: users are guided by voice and a demo video, interacting with the MFL to receive feedback on the 3D game view. Once familiar with activating ADM, users can tackle more challenging tasks, learning to use the "set" and "minus/plus" buttons to control speed.

Mission 3: Deactivate and Resume

This project innovates a gamified assisted driving training experience being developed with Unreal Engine 5, it fully integrates into both development and existing BMW system for hardware and software. Showcased in a real car mock-up, it features responsive interactions to driving inputs including braking and accelerator pedal, hands-on detection and steering wheel buttons.
