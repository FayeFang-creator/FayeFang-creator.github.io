"NIPA": BMW Multifaceted AI Agents

The research introduces an innovative conceptual framework for human-AI collaboration, exploring the potential for conversational AI agents to adaptively embody diverse personalities that cater to users' preferences. Consequently, a multimodal prototype was developed, featuring a cutting-edge AI-driven intelligent system constructed on a 1:1 mock-up with central display and pillar-to-pillar head-up display.

background

BMW is actively exploring the future potential of its in-car conversational agent, focusing on creating a "human-like" AI rather than a mere "tool-like" one. Drawing from established research, a critical question emerges: "What type of agent can best fulfill the diverse needs of users from different backgrounds and contexts?" The innovative solution lies in developing a multifaceted personalized agent with flexible capabilities to serve users in various scenarios. To achieve this, both theoretical and empirical research is dedicated to defining AI personalities that align with users' habits, harnessing the power of large language models to enhance the overall user experience.

research questions

The research focuses on two key questions: first, how to establish a theoretical framework for defining a personalized AI agent with multifaceted personalities, and second, how users mentally differentiate these personalities and interact with them flexibly in everyday in-car scenarios. This research-through-design project proposes a conceptual framework that serves as a typography for fine-tuning large language models (LLMs, such as GPT-4o) using specific inputs to define and distinguish the human-like characteristics of multifaceted AI personalities. Consequently, three fundamental aspects are detailed to map these human-like features: native character (including vocal timbre and physical appearance), communicative style, and behavior.
