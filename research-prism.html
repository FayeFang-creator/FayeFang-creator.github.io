Involved with multi-stakeholders with Politecnico research team, NGO, local business owners, local residents and community center, etc, we conducted a gamified, handmade booklet with stickers to encourage residents to engage with their community and propose ideas for transformation.

VISION

Design Goal

For decades, Milan has been exploring resilient strategies to tackle environmental, social, and economic challenges through collaborative actions involving citizens and policy maker. In recent years, the European Commission has played an essential role in supporting sustainable transition, calling for an increasing involvement of people in development processes.  Adriano is a experimental community that has been explored and implemented with co-design activities to involve local residents, business and designers together to renovate vacant and abandoned spaces.

"Multi-Stakeholders"

A multi-disciplinary research and design team, is launched to explore the models towards sustainability, livability and well-being. “A Community Centered Design” has been started to explore methodologies and design frameworks for co-design process to tackle urban problems. That means a process to involve diverse stakeholders: local residents, urban planners, experts and engineers, business owner and the government managers to design and build the community together.

Design approach

Department of Design in Politecnico di Milano proposed a theoretical model of service design thinking to tackle the development of urban problems with multi-disciplinary thinking and stakeholders. Regarding Residents/citizens as the centered users, politicians, organizers, local business owners are involved to form the service ecosystem. The design process is based on the theory.

Desktop research

Macro Perspective:
An Fragmented, Disconnected Community

Adriano is a medium-density and moderately urbanized neighborhood in Milan, with fairly new development area located at the northeastern border of the municipality. The area is bordered at the south of city Monza and Cologno Monzese, also adjacent to the popular canal Naviglio Martesana. Besides residential neighborhood with ample green space, it’s also home to some corporate headquarters, such as Siemens Italia. However, due to long-term engineering stagnation and failure of operation of the real estate, Adriano is facing unpredictable future of urban development. Our research team starts from the urban scope and observed “disconnection” as the main issues that stops Adriano from growing to be a well-being and sustainable community.

Micro Perspective:
The Fresh, Diverse, Well-Educated Residents

However, Adriano's demographic analysis shows a thriving trend: over the past decade, Adriano's population has surged by 46%, attracting younger, well-educated residents with families due to its high-quality living conditions and lower housing prices compared to the rest of Milan.

User research

Demographic Research: 
The New "Mid Class"

Our secondary research revealed that Adriano's growing population represents a "New Mid Class," characterized by stable economic and psychological conditions, including being younger, healthier, well-educated, and ambitious. To further understand their needs, we launched an online questionnaire in a Facebook group focused on Adriano, receiving 120 responses between February and April 2020, which helped us summarize the traits of this emerging demographic.

Persona & Focus Group

The demographic research reveals the emerging persona in Adriano community, which values higher education, healthy lifestyles, and diverse skills, seeking self-expression through activities like drama and robotics workshops in Adriano. Insights from interviews reveal a demand for engaging, low-cost initiatives that offer growth value, while experts stress the need for community integration, environmentally friendly practices, and flexible reuse of public spaces.

Research-through-design questions

Problems, Opportunities

The central challenge is to "create pathways for users to actively engage in community building while overcoming social barriers," focusing on the dual objectives of "empowerment" and "connection."

The Metaphor: "Prism"

"Prism" serves as a metaphor for its dual functions of "Reflection and Refraction," embodying a visionary strategy to connect the community and empower residents. On one hand, connection involves fostering social interaction and vibrancy through public service, facilities, and space design; on the other, empowerment focuses on delivering training, knowledge, and resources that support personal growth and achievement.

"Prism": The Action Plan

Service system planning involved identifying community issues and potential activity spaces, followed by brainstorming and initiating project plans, refining those plans, and ultimately gathering feedback.

"Prism": Service Ecosystem

The "Prism" service encompasses a diverse range of stakeholders within the ecosystem, including the "Prism" online platform, target residents, local business owners, strategic planning and space design experts, government agencies, community institutions, NGOs, and local organizations.

"Prism": Service Blueprint

prototype

Participantory Prototyping

The handbook involves local residents to use maps and stickers to uncover intriguing local spots, like nearby parks, by selecting stickers and placing them on a designated book.

The process begins with an announcement in the Facebook community, encouraging residents to form teams for collaboration. Next, a discussion will be facilitated at the community center to explore ways to integrate ideas effectively, followed by advanced planning based on these discussions. Red stickers will be utilized to indicate practical methods and principles related to various spaces, tools, activities, organizations, and layouts. Throughout this process, thorough documentation will be maintained, and regular updates will be shared on Facebook to keep the community informed of progress and developments.

The tester facilitated a community discussion, leading to the decision to create an open-air cinema in the parking lot at the entrance. Using sustainable methods, they proposed dividing the space with painted brush markings and reached out to the local ice cream parlor for sponsorship.

conclusion

The Adriano Community Center successfully established its own Facebook page, assisting 56 community participants in initiating a variety of activities. Our service system has proven to be a valuable reference tool and has significantly promoted community engagement.
